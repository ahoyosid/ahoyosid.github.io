Andrés Hoyos-Idrobo

Machine learning · optimal transport · learning to rank · causal inference

I'm a Senior Research Scientist at Rakuten Group, Inc. in Paris, in the Search & Relevance team. Before that, I worked on recommender systems at the Rakuten Institute of Technology. My recent work covers optimal transport, learning to rank and counterfactual evaluation for e-commerce search, and bias correction for treatment-effect estimation.

I did my PhD at Inria (Parietal team) and Université Paris-Saclay, building fast and stable statistical learning methods for large-scale brain imaging (fMRI). More about me →
